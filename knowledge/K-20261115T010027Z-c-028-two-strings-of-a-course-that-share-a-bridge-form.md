With an exact unison and equal plucks the antisymmetric mode is not excited and no aftersound appears in a one-polarisation model; the model therefore keeps a small default mistuning. Round 3: the coupling in the spike is purely resistive and one polarisation is modelled; with eta_b = 2e-3 at 660 Hz the frequencies lock below about 3.5 cents of mistuning; a reactive bridge admittance repels the two frequencies instead of locking them.
