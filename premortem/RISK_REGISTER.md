# Risk register

Severity by the rubric in `HANDOFF.md` section 6. Likelihood: `expected` (happens on the planned path unless something changes), `plausible` (needs one named condition the plan does not exclude), `remote` (needs several independent conditions). Status: `mitigated`, `accepted`, `open`, `speculative` (no failure path found; not counted). "Origin" says where the risk was first recorded.
